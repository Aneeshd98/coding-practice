Subthalamic Lab · anatomical model credits

brain.bin / brain.json (cerebral hemispheres, cerebellum, brainstem and head outline)

These surfaces are adapted from the anatomical meshes distributed with Cortex Atlas
(https://github.com/jeetblahiri/eeg-learn, public/anatomy/cortex.mesh and scalp.mesh),
which that project distributes under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0.
Their sources, as credited there:

- Cerebral surfaces: adapted from the Human Atlas 3D head by Dannysyed
  (https://github.com/Dannysyed/human-atlas-face), derived from Z-Anatomy
  (https://www.z-anatomy.com/) and BodyParts3D, © The Database Center for Life
  Science (DBCLS) (https://lifesciencedb.jp/bp3d/).
- Head outline: "Infinite, 3D Head Scan" by Lee Perry-Smith, Infinite-Realities /
  Triplegangers, Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

Changes made for Subthalamic Lab (scripts/prepare_anatomy.py): the cerebral surface was
split into its connected parts; small parts lying entirely within the deep core were
removed; the remaining parts were grouped into left and right cerebrum, cerebellum and
brainstem; coordinates were rescaled to millimetres in an MNI-like frame fitted to the
cerebral bounding box; and vertices were re-quantized.

The adapted surfaces in brain.bin and brain.json are distributed under Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

mri.bin / mri.json (MRI slice in the 3D view)

A cropped, brain-masked, 8-bit copy of the ICBM 152 Nonlinear Symmetrical 2009c T1-weighted
template (Fonov et al., NeuroImage 2011; Fonov et al., NeuroImage 2009; Collins et al. 1999),
as distributed by TemplateFlow (tpl-MNI152NLin2009cSym, res-1). Prepared with
scripts/prepare_mri.py: cropped to x -60..60, y -60..40, z -40..50 mm, voxels outside the
template's brain mask set to 0, and intensities rescaled to 1-255.

Copyright (C) 1993-2004 Louis Collins, McConnell Brain Imaging Centre, Montreal
Neurological Institute, McGill University. The full notice is in MNI152-LICENSE.txt in
this folder. The template is an average of many brains; the brain surfaces and the
schematic nuclei are only approximately aligned to it.

Deep structures (STN, globus pallidus, putamen, caudate, thalamus, red nucleus,
substantia nigra) and the DBS leads are original schematic shapes generated in code
(src/three/anatomy.ts) from rounded textbook positions. They are not an atlas and
must not be used for surgical planning or targeting.
