One minute of resting subthalamic LFP from each condition (OFF and ON levodopa) of participant
sub-2IhVOz in:

  Rassoulou F, Steina A, Hartmann CJ, Vesper J, Butz M, Schnitzler A, Hirschmann J.
  Exploring the electrophysiology of Parkinson's disease with magnetoencephalography and
  deep brain recordings. Scientific Data 11, 889 (2024). https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-024-03768-1
  Dataset: OpenNeuro ds004998 v1.2.2, doi:10.18112/openneuro.ds004998.v1.2.2

The dataset is released under CC0 1.0. Only the eight DBS contacts were kept; the excerpts
were filtered and downsampled as described in ds004998-sub-2IhVOz.json and
scripts/prepare_recording.py. The selection of this participant, and how many participants
were screened, are recorded in the same manifest.
